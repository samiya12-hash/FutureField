# 🌱 FutureField

### From Earth Observation to Better Farm Decisions

FutureField is a **NASA-data-powered decision-support platform** that helps farmers make smarter, climate-resilient crop and crop-rotation decisions.

By combining **NASA Earth observations** such as **SMAP soil moisture, GPM rainfall, and MODIS/VIIRS temperature and vegetation data** with local soil information, crop requirements, and farmer priorities, FutureField analyzes farm conditions, evaluates crop suitability, and generates **multi-season crop-rotation strategies**.

Farmers can also explore **what-if scenarios**, understand why recommendations are made, and assess their field's **resilience to climate risks**. Through continuous farmer feedback, the system can improve its recommendations over time.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 🛰️ **NASA Earth Observation Data** — SMAP, GPM, MODIS/VIIRS
* 🌱 **Farm Condition Analysis** — soil moisture, rainfall, temperature, vegetation health
* 🌾 **Crop Suitability Analysis** — evaluate which crops are suitable for a field
* 🔄 **Crop Rotation Planning** — generate multi-season rotation strategies
* 📊 **Explainable Recommendations** — understand why a crop or strategy is recommended
* 🌦️ **What-If Simulation** — explore changes in rainfall, temperature, and water availability
* 🛡️ **Field Resilience Score** — assess resilience to climate-related risks
* 👨‍🌾 **Farmer Feedback Loop** — improve recommendations using real-world outcomes

## 🌍 Vision

FutureField aims to bridge the gap between **NASA Earth observations and practical farm decision-making**, helping farmers move from **reactive farming to proactive, climate-resilient planning**.
