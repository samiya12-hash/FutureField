FutureField is a NASA-data-powered decision-support platform that helps farmers make smarter, climate-resilient crop and crop-rotation decisions. By combining NASA Earth observations such as SMAP soil moisture, GPM rainfall, and MODIS/VIIRS temperature and vegetation data with local soil information, crop requirements, and farmer priorities, FutureField analyzes farm conditions, evaluates crop suitability, and generates multi-season rotation strategies. Farmers can also explore what-if scenarios, understand why recommendations are made, and assess their field’s resilience to climate risks. Through continuous farmer feedback, the system can improve its recommendations over time.
